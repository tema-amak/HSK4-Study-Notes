## Grammar 1：正好 zhènghǎo

**Meaning**: two usages: ① adj: _just right_ (size, number, time, amount, fits perfectly) ② adv: _by lucky chance; happen to (be doing sth)_

### Usage 1 — adj. just‑right

Structure: Noun + 正好 (+ number /description)

> Textbook example: 苹果八块五，西瓜十一块五，**正好**二十块。 Apples 8.5 yuan, watermelon 11.5 yuan; exactly 20 yuan total.

Daily example:

> 这件衣服大小**正好**。This shirt’s size is just right.

### Usage 2 — adv. happen‑to (lucky opportunity)

Structure: Sentence, + 正好 + action

> Textbook example: 我们下午要去踢足球，**正好**一起去吧。 We are going to play football this afternoon, come along with us.

Daily example:

> 我要去邮局，**正好**可以帮你寄信。 I’m going to the post office; I can send your letter for you.

❌ Mistake: Do NOT use 正好 for bad luck situations. Wrong: _正好我生病了。_ Right: **不巧** I happened to get sick. (不巧 = unfortunately it so happened)

✅ Quick practice: 你来得______，我刚想找你。(answer → 正好)

## Grammar 2：差不多 chàbuduō

> Can be **adjective OR adverb**. Means almost, nearly; similar, not much different.

### Usage 1: Adjective (predicate / before noun): similar

Structure: A 跟 B + 差不多

> Textbook example: 他们俩性格**差不多**。Their personalities are quite similar.

Daily: 这两个杯子**差不多**。These two cups are about the same.

### Usage 2: Adverb: nearly, almost (quantity, time)

Structure: 差不多 + number‑measure word

> Textbook example: 来多少人？ —— **差不多**一半儿吧。 How many people are coming? ‑ Almost half.

Daily: 我**差不多**每天七点起床。I get up almost every day at 7.

### 🆚 Compare: 差不多 VS 几乎 jīhū (very important HSK4 test point)

表格

||差不多 chàbuduō|几乎 jīhū|
|---|---|---|
|Word class|adj + adv|only adverb|
|Before numbers|✅ 差不多 50 岁|❌ cannot put before numbers|
|Short single‑syllable adj|✅ 差不多高|❌ must add extra word →几乎一样高|
|Near‑miss (something bad nearly happened, but DID NOT happen)|❌ cannot use|✅ 我几乎忘了。I almost forgot.|
|End‑of‑sentence predicate|✅ 性格差不多。|❌ cannot end sentence|

Examples of contrast: ✅ 他**差不多**四十岁。(number → use 差不多) ✅ 我**几乎**迟到了。(bad thing nearly happened → use 几乎)

❌ Wrong: 他们俩几乎高。 ✅ Right: 他们俩差不多高。 / 他们俩几乎一样高。

> Self‑check exercise:

1. __________ 三公斤。 → **差不多**
2. 我 __________ 错过了公交车。 → **几乎**

## Grammar 3：尽管 jǐnguǎn (conjunction)

> Meaning: **although / even though**. Used for **real, true facts (not hypothetical)**. Shows contrast: Fact A is true, but result B still happens.

✅ Sentence structure:

> **尽管 + factual situation**, [但是 / 可是 / 却 / 还是] + contrasting result

> Textbook example: **尽管**已经毕业这么多年，我们**还是**经常联系的。 Although we graduated many years ago, we still keep in touch often.

Another textbook example:

> **尽管**现在工作很忙，**可**到周末他还是会去打球。 Even though he is very busy with work now, he still plays ball on weekends.

Daily‑life example:

> **尽管**外面下雨，我**还是**要出门。 Although it is raining outside, I still need to go out.

⚠️ Notes: 1. 尽管 describes facts that really exist. Do NOT use for imaginary “if‑scenarios”. (For hypothetical “even if” use **即使…… 也……** from Lesson 1!)

> 📌 Critical difference recap:
> 
> - **尽管**: real fact → _Although it is raining (it is really raining)_, I go out.
> - **即使**: hypothetical → _Even if it rains (maybe it won’t rain)_, I will go out.

2. The second‑clause connector (但是 / 可是 / 还是) can NOT be omitted most of the time in HSK4 exercises.

❌ Wrong: 尽管很累，我去上课。 ✅ Right: 尽管很累，我**还是**去上课。

## Grammar 4：却 què

> Meaning: but /yet, soft‑mild contrast (weaker tone than 但是). It is an **adverb**, NOT a conjunction!

✅ Word‑order rule: **却 always goes AFTER subject, BEFORE verb**.

> Subject + 却 + Verb / Adjective

Textbook example:

> 一个脾气不好的人虽然不一定让人讨厌，但是**却**很难跟人交朋友。 Someone with a bad temper is not necessarily disliked, yet it is hard for them to make friends.

Daily example:

> 他看上去很年轻，**却**已经五十岁了。 He looks very young, yet he is already fifty years old.

❌ Common mistake (wrong word‑order): _却他很忙。_ ✅ Correct: 他**却**很忙。

💡 Compare 却 vs 但是：

- **但是**: conjunction, sits at the START of second sentence.

> 他很忙，**但是**他愿意帮我。

- **却**: adverb, sits after subject, before verb.

> 他很忙，**却**愿意帮我。

> You can even combine them together: 但是 … 却 … (seen in textbook sentences)

## Grammar 5：而 ér

> Conjunction. Two main HSK 4 uses: **contrast** (while), or simple connection “and”.

### Usage 1: Contrast “while” (compare two different people / ideas / situations)

Structure: Sentence A，**而** Sentence B

> Textbook example: 有些人觉得朋友就是能和自己一起快乐的人，**而**我的理解是：当你遇到困难的时候，真正的朋友会站出来。 Some people think friends are people you have fun with, while my understanding is: true friends step forward when you face difficulty.

Daily example:

> 年轻人喜欢热闹，**而**老年人喜欢安静。 Young people like excitement, while elderly people like quietness.

### Usage 2: Simple coordination “and” (written‑style, connect two related facts)

> Textbook example: “明” 字，左边的 “日” 意思是太阳，**而**右边的 “月” 意思是月亮。 The character ming: the left 日 means sun, and the right 月 means moon.

⚠️ Notes about 而: 1. Most HSK‑4 exam questions test its **contrast meaning (while)**. 2. Do NOT over‑use 而 in spoken daily chat, it leans toward written /textbook Chinese. In casual speech prefer 可是 / 但是.
