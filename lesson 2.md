# Lesson 2 Vocabulary 生词

## 1. 适应

**pinyin:** shìyìng  
**word type:** 动词 (verb)  
**meaning:** to adapt to; to get used to

### Structure:

**适应 + noun**

Examples:

- 适应生活  
    → to adapt to life
    
- 适应环境  
    → to adapt to the environment
    
- 适应新的工作  
    → to get used to a new job
    

### Common expressions:

- 适应中国的生活  
    → adapt to life in China
    
- 慢慢适应  
    → gradually get used to
    

Examples:

1. 我刚来中国的时候不习惯，现在已经慢慢适应了。  
    → When I first came to China, I wasn't used to it. Now I have gradually adapted.
    
2. 你适应这里的生活了吗？  
    → Have you adapted to life here?

# 13. 好像

**pinyin:** hǎoxiàng  
**word type:** 副词 / 动词

**Meaning:** seem; appear; as if

## Usage 1: "seem to be"

### Structure:

**好像 + verb/adjective**

Examples:

- 他好像不认识我。  
    → He seems not to know me.

## Usage 2: "as if"

Examples:

- 她好像很了解我。  
    → She seems to understand me very well.
    
- 他说话的声音好像老师。  
    → His voice sounds like a teacher's.
    

### Common expressions:

- 好像是……  
    → It seems to be...
    
- 好像没有……  
    → It seems there isn't...

# 15. 尽管

**pinyin:** jǐnguǎn  
**word type:** 连词

**Meaning:** although; even though

### Structure:

**尽管 A，但是 B**

Meaning:  
Although A, B still happens.

Examples:

- 尽管天气很冷，但是他每天跑步。  
    → Although the weather is cold, he runs every day.
    
- 尽管工作很忙，他还是来看我。  
    → Although he is busy, he still came to see me.
    

### Important:

In Chinese, "但是/可是/可是" is often used after 尽管.

# 26. # 而

**Pinyin:** ér  
**Part of speech:** conjunction  
**Meaning:** and; while; but

It is used to connect two parts of a sentence and show a relationship between them.

### Showing contrast (while / whereas)

### Structure:

**A，而 B**

Meaning:  
A is one situation, while B is different.

Examples:

- 我喜欢安静，而他喜欢热闹。  
    → I like quiet places, while he likes lively places.
- 南方天气比较暖，而北方天气比较冷。  
    → The weather in the south is warmer, while the north is colder.

---

## 2. Showing a change or difference (but)

It can be similar to **但是 (but)**, but **而** is more formal.

Examples:

- 他很年轻，而经验很丰富。  
    → He is young, but his experience is very rich.
- 这个问题看起来简单，而实际上很困难。  
    → This problem looks simple, but actually it is difficult.

---

## Difference between 而 and 却

### 却 (què)

Focuses on an **unexpected result**.

Example:

- 他很年轻，却很有经验。  
    → He is young, yet he has a lot of experience.

(The speaker thinks young people usually lack experience, so this is surprising.)

---

### 而 (ér)

Focuses on **comparison or difference between two things**.

Example:

- 他喜欢运动，而我喜欢看书。  
    → He likes sports, while I like reading.

(No surprise, just a difference.)
